## How to Use the Website (Step-by-Step Guide Covered in the Demo Video)

1. **Explore the Standalone 3D Landing Page (`/`):**
   - Switch between **Standalone Villa (3F)**, **Residential Tower (8F)**, and **Commercial Core (5F)** to preview real-time Three.js architectural massing and achieved FSI.
   - Scroll to **Architecture** and **Parameters** to inspect all 7 backend feature clusters (`C1–C7`), 8 quiz underwriting gates (`Q1–Q8`), and spatial holdout ML cohorts.
2. **Locate Any Property (`Explore a Property` -> Workspace 02):**
   - Pick a Pan-India/Global metro preset or enter custom GPS coordinates (`latitude`, `longitude`) to resolve municipal base land rates, circle-rate floors, and medical priority zone adjustments.
3. **Complete the Interactive 8-Gate Quiz (`Proceed to Property Quiz`):**
   - Step through Asset Classification, Plot vs Built-Up Footprint, CPWD Construction Grade, Fit-Out & Parking, Micro-Market Frontage, Flood/Drainage/Water-Table Environmental Gates, Freehold Title Clearance, and Monthly Rent Calibration.
4. **Inspect the 3D Parcel & Run Valuation (`Execute Full Valuation`):**
   - Preview floor-by-floor 3D massing (`Inspect 3D Model`) and generate the reconciled **Fair Market Value**, lower/upper quantile range, Stage 1 Land vs Stage 2 Depreciated Structure, Out-of-Distribution flags, and TreeSHAP attributions.
5. **Analyze Rental Yields, Market Drivers & Export Reports:**
   - Use **Rental Intelligence** for NOI/Cap Rate sensitivity sliders, **Market Analytics** for live SHAP evaluations, **Comparable Evidence** for SHA-256 verified deed auditing, and **Reports** to download HTML Dossiers or structured JSON ledgers.
